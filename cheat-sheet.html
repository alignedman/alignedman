The Aligned Ultimate Fat Burning & Muscle Preservation Stack

Retatrutide · Tesamorelin · 5-Amino-1MQ

The most advanced fat-loss protocol in The Aligned Man arsenal. Retatrutide is a triple-receptor agonist (GLP-1, GIP, Glucagon) that drives aggressive fat loss while actively preserving lean muscle mass. Tesamorelin specifically targets visceral abdominal fat while elevating GH to protect muscle during the cut. 5-Amino-1MQ inhibits NNMT, unlocking stored fat at the cellular level.

Retatrutide (GLP-3)
DosageStart 0.5–1mg/week · Titrate to 4–8mg/week
Cycle12–24 weeks · Titrate slowly
Spectrum3.0
Next-generation triple agonist beyond semaglutide. Activates GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. Only if prescribed/monitored.
Tesamorelin
Dosage1–2mg daily · Subcutaneous injection
Cycle8–12 weeks on · 4 weeks off
Spectrum3.5
GHRH analog targeting visceral abdominal fat — the dangerous deep belly fat. Elevates GH to shred visceral fat and protect lean muscle simultaneously. The gut goes first on this compound.
5-Amino-1MQ
Dosage50–100mg daily
Cycle8 weeks on · 2 weeks off
Spectrum3.0
NNMT inhibitor that unlocks stored fat at the cellular level by elevating NAD+ and activating metabolic pathways. Synergistic with Retatrutide and Tesamorelin.
